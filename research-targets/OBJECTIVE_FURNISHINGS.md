# Objective furnishings target inventory

This is a controlled research queue, not a finished history. Each target
should eventually be partitioned by period and geography and supplied with
proper terms, definitions, visual features, materials, source requirements,
image requirements, and a priority.

## Pilot families

| Family | Included targets | Pilot status |
|---|---|---|
| Furniture | chairs, tables, cabinets, credenzas, desks, beds | unstarted |
| Lighting | chandeliers, sconces, table lamps, floor lamps | unstarted |
| Plumbing | sink basins, faucets, bathtubs, vanities | unstarted |
| Surface treatments | paint, wallpaper, tile, stone, wood paneling | unstarted |
| Decorative objects | curios, mirrors, ceramics, clocks, hardware | unstarted |

## Priority queue

### P0 — durable and visually diagnostic in modern homes

Furniture, lamps, sink basins, faucets, bathtubs, vanities, paint, wallpaper,
curios, and credenzas.

### P1 — diagnostic components and variants

Cabriole and other legs, feet, handles, escutcheons, basin profiles, faucet
mechanisms, moldings, paneling, tile patterns, finishes, and construction
details.

### P2 — specialist or primarily museum-held targets

Rare subtypes, highly localized variants, and objects whose identification
requires specialist evidence.

## Matrix axes

### Period bands

1. 1450–1600 — Renaissance / early modern
2. 1600–1750 — Baroque / Dutch Golden Age
3. 1750–1830 — Rococo, Neoclassical, Georgian
4. 1830–1900 — Victorian and Arts & Crafts precursors
5. 1900–1945 — Art Nouveau, Arts & Crafts, Art Deco, modernism
6. 1945–1980 — postwar and mid-century modern
7. 1980–present — postmodern, contemporary, and revival styles

### Geography buckets

Britain; France; Italy; German-speaking Europe; Netherlands/Scandinavia;
Iberia/Mediterranean; Ottoman/Islamic traditions; East Asia; South Asia;
North America; and Latin America.

Each cell is marked `unstarted`, `partial`, or `researched`. The first pilot
uses the three broad period buckets and three geography buckets defined in
`CONVERGENCE_PLAN.md`; this larger matrix remains the expansion map.

## Required target entry

For each target, record:

- canonical proper name and synonyms;
- dictionary or institutional definition;
- object/component family and typical room/use;
- diagnostic shape, construction, material, and surface features;
- period and geography hypotheses with confidence;
- related styles and confusable terms;
- modern-home survivability;
- authoritative source targets and image/rights requirements;
- priority, matrix status, unresolved questions, and next action.
